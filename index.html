A SIMPLE RULE

Never experiment with the only copy of a save.

Before changing folders, replacing files, moving between installations, or testing a compatibility fix, make a separate backup. It takes much less time than reconstructing lost progress.

This site focuses on practical file-management habits rather than promising that every save will work across every version or platform.
